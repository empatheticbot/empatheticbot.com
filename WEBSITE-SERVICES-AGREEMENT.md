## 2. Services

### 2.1 Included Services

Provider will perform the following services for Client’s website (collectively, the **“Services”**):

1. Website strategy, design, and development consistent with the Project Brief;
2. Basic managed hosting while the subscription is active;
3. Registration and renewal of one standard domain, DNS configuration, and reasonable domain administration while the subscription is active;
4. Setup and reasonable administration of Client email and other third-party service accounts;
5. Ongoing content updates, including changes to hours, photos, copy, menus, and similar website content;
6. Security patches and software maintenance;
7. Routine performance and accessibility testing and tuning;
8. Basic technical and on-page search-engine setup;
9. Basic website analytics setup and reporting;
10. New pages and modest features that the Parties plan into the ongoing work; and
11. Reasonable consultation and support relating to the website.

Provider will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner. Provider will generally respond to Client communications within two business days, although a response time is not a guaranteed completion time.

### 2.2 Request Queue and Scope

Client may submit an unlimited number of requests, but Provider will ordinarily work on **one active request at a time**. Provider will prioritize requests with Client and complete them at a commercially reasonable pace. Small requests will often be completed within a few business days, but timing depends on complexity, Client feedback, third-party systems, and Provider’s availability.

The subscription is designed for small-business marketing and service websites. Unless the Parties sign a written amendment, it does not include:

- a full online store or custom e-commerce system;
- a complex web application;
- custom software materially beyond the website described in the Project Brief;
- high-volume content entry, original photography, video production, or extensive original copywriting;
- legal, accounting, advertising, or regulatory-compliance services; or
- work for a separate brand, business, or website.

If a request is outside this scope, Provider will explain that before beginning the request. The Parties may defer it, replace another planned item with it, or agree to a separate written scope and price. Provider will not charge an additional fee without Client’s prior written approval.

### 2.3 Project Brief

The Parties will document the initial website goals, pages, features, brand inputs, and known integrations in **Exhibit A (Project Brief)**. Email or GitHub communications may clarify day-to-day details, but they do not amend the Subscription Fee, Initial Term, ownership provisions, or other material terms unless both Parties expressly agree in writing to an amendment.

## 4. Fees and Payment

### 4.1 Subscription Fee

Client will pay Provider **$200 per month in advance**, with the first payment due on the Effective Date and each later payment due on the same calendar day of each month. If a month has no corresponding calendar day, payment is due on that month’s final day. The Subscription Fee is fixed throughout the Initial Term.

The Initial Term is a twelve-month commitment with a total minimum Subscription Fee of $2,400. Monthly billing is a payment schedule and does not make the Initial Term cancelable month-to-month.

### 4.2 Taxes and Third-Party Costs

The Subscription Fee does not include sales, use, or similar taxes that Provider is legally required to collect. Client is responsible for those taxes, excluding taxes on Provider’s net income.

The Subscription Fee includes the ordinary registration and renewal charges for one standard domain selected for Client’s website. A **“standard domain”** is a non-premium domain available from Provider’s chosen registrar at its ordinary registration and renewal pricing. Client is responsible for premium-domain purchase or renewal charges, domain-broker or aftermarket charges, additional domains, premium registrar features, email services, paid software, stock assets, transaction fees, advertising charges, and other third-party vendor costs. Provider may administer the included domain or basic hosting through a Provider-controlled vendor account during the subscription. Other vendor accounts will be opened or maintained in Client’s name wherever reasonably possible, use Client’s payment method, and remain subject to the applicable vendor’s terms. Provider will obtain Client’s approval before adding a paid service.

### 4.3 Late Payment and Suspension

If a payment is more than 10 calendar days late, Provider may suspend work and, after giving Client written notice, may suspend managed hosting until the account is current. Provider will use reasonable efforts to avoid an abrupt public-site interruption. A suspension for nonpayment does not extend the Initial Term or waive the unpaid amount.

Client must raise a good-faith billing dispute in writing within 15 days after the disputed charge. The Parties will work promptly to resolve it, and Client will timely pay all undisputed amounts.

### 4.4 Pricing After the Initial Term

Provider may change the Subscription Fee after the Initial Term by giving Client at least 90 days’ written notice. Client may reject the change by terminating the Agreement before the new price takes effect.

## 5. Term and Termination

### 5.1 Initial Term and Renewal

The Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the 12-month Initial Term. It then automatically continues month-to-month unless terminated under this Section.

### 5.2 Cancellation After the Initial Term

After the Initial Term, either Party may terminate the Agreement for any reason by giving the other Party at least 30 days’ written notice. Client remains responsible for Subscription Fees through the effective termination date. Fees for a partial billing month are not prorated.

### 5.3 Ending the Agreement During the Initial Term

Client may ask to end the relationship during the Initial Term, but the Initial Term is noncancelable except for Provider’s uncured material breach under Section 5.4 or a written early-termination agreement signed by both Parties. Unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing, all unpaid monthly Subscription Fees for the remainder of the Initial Term become due when an early termination requested by Client takes effect.

The Parties may agree in writing to a different, fair wind-down arrangement based on the circumstances. Provider is not required to agree to a reduction of the remaining Initial Term fees.

### 5.4 Termination for Material Breach

Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 15 days after receiving written notice describing the breach. If a breach cannot reasonably be cured within 15 days, the breaching Party will not be in default if it begins curing the breach within that period and diligently completes the cure.

Provider may terminate immediately if Client uses the Services for unlawful activity, creates a material security risk, or repeatedly requests publication of unlawful or infringing material.

If Client properly terminates because of Provider’s uncured material breach, Client owes no Subscription Fees for periods after termination and no accelerated fees for the remainder of the Initial Term.

### 5.5 Effect of Termination and Offboarding

When this Agreement ends, and after Client has paid all undisputed amounts then due, Provider will, within a commercially reasonable time:

1. Transfer the website’s GitHub repository to the GitHub user or organization designated by Client, to the extent GitHub permits the transfer; or, if a direct transfer is not reasonably available, deliver a current repository archive with available commit history;
2. Work with Client to transfer the domain registration and DNS administration to the registrar account designated by Client, including providing any reasonably required authorization or transfer code;
3. Work with Client to move the website from Provider’s managed hosting to hosting designated by Client, or transfer the relevant hosting account or project if the vendor permits it;
4. Deliver the complete custom source code, Client-provided assets in Provider’s possession, and reasonable deployment notes;
5. Provide up to two hours of reasonable transition assistance during the 30 days following termination; and
6. Remove Provider’s access from Client-controlled accounts and, after confirming the transfers, remove Provider’s retained credentials and access to the transferred repository, domain, and hosting, except for archival copies Provider must retain by law or for ordinary business records.

Client is responsible for designating and purchasing any replacement hosting, registrar, and other replacement services. The Parties will use reasonable efforts to begin the transfer during the 30-day notice period and avoid unnecessary website downtime. If reasonably necessary for an orderly transfer, Provider will keep the existing basic hosting available for up to 30 days after the effective termination date, provided Client’s account is current and continued hosting does not create a legal or security risk. This limited transition hosting does not extend the subscription or include ongoing website work. Any transition help beyond the included two hours requires a separate written agreement.

Sections that by their nature should continue—including payment, ownership, confidentiality, warranty disclaimers, indemnification, limitations of liability, dispute terms, and general terms—survive termination.
