Privacy notice

Effective July 18, 2026

When you contact empatheticbot, you’re trusting me with information about you and your business. I use it to understand your inquiry and reply—not to build an advertising profile or fill a marketing list.

Information I collect

The contact form may collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your business name and current website
  • What kind of website help you need
  • Your goals, timing, and anything else you include in your message

The site and its security services may also process ordinary technical information, such as your IP address, browser details, and request metadata, to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems.

How I use it

I use this information to respond to you, evaluate whether we’re a good fit, prepare requested proposals or agreements, protect the contact form, and keep reasonable business records. I do not sell your information. I will not add you to a marketing list without separate permission.

Who helps process it

I use service providers to host and protect the website and to deliver email. They may process information only as needed to provide those services and under their own terms and privacy obligations.

How long I keep it

I keep inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential or active business relationship, maintain ordinary business records, and meet legal obligations. I may delete inquiries that do not lead to a working relationship sooner.

Your choices

You may ask what information I have about you or ask me to correct or delete it, subject to records I reasonably need to retain. Email hello@empatheticbot.com with a privacy question or request.
