Independent website reviews

Every review, in one place.

Each of these was written for one organization, unasked and unpaid: what a visitor runs into on their site, what it costs them, and what a clearer version could look like. Every page is unlisted and stays that way — they are meant to be read by the people they were written for.

  • Canton Center Animal Hospital September 30, 2026

    A website that fits the phone.

    The site is fast and it is secure—two things that are genuinely hard to fix later. Its largest constraint is that it was built for a desktop monitor and never told a phone what to do with it, so a visitor on a phone sees the whole page drawn at about a third of readable size.

  • Affordable Dry Basement September 30, 2026

    Say where you are, and what you fix.

    The site is well built underneath—high accessibility and best-practice scores, and nothing jumps around as it loads. Its largest constraint is that a stranger cannot tell from the first screen what they do or where, and the proof they have earned everywhere else is not on the page.

    Read the review →

  • Integrity Landscape, LLC September 30, 2026

    A portfolio you can actually look at.

    The site is fast, stable, and better organised than almost anything its age. Its largest constraint is that the phone gets a different, much smaller website—one with no photographs of their work on it—and that neither version lets a visitor zoom in.
